# Sources

What the coaching rules rest on. The tags ([S1], …) are used in `methodology.md`,
`analysis.md`, `workouts.md`, `strength.md` and `checkin-and-race.md`. Rules are written in the
skill's own words; nothing here is a substitute for reading the papers.

**Evidence strength:** **strong** = meta-analysis, large cohort or RCT with consistent results;
**moderate** = smaller or mixed studies, consensus statements; **practice** = coaching convention
or extrapolation without direct trials. When a runner asks "why?", say which kind it is.

Citations were checked against PubMed, the journal or the full text in October 2026. DOIs marked
"not checked" were not verified independently.

## Conventions without a single source

The 7-zone Z1/Z2/X/Z3/Y/Z4/Z5 model (80/20 Endurance, Matt Fitzgerald; as TrainingPeaks
calculates it) and its percentages, easy running at 70–80 % of threshold speed,
session types (cruise intervals, tempo, VO2 reps), the race-to-threshold factors in `paces.py`,
and the periodization phase lengths are standard coaching practice. They're reasonable defaults,
not research findings. Say so if asked.
