The quality sessions are threshold or sub-threshold work (e.g. cruise intervals or tempo at about threshold pace ~4:45/km) and/or long intervals near 5k pace with reps of about 2-5 minutes; it does not rely on short sprints (e.g. 30-second reps) as the main quality, and paces are given in min/km or by heart rate.
