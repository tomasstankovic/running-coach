Fuelling during the race adds up to about 45 g carbs per hour (about 70 g in all, not more than the 45 g/h the runner tolerated in training): for example 2 gels of 25 g plus about 20-25 g from sports drink, with drinking to thirst (more in the heat, with sodium). A plan of 2 gels alone (about 30 g/h) without saying where the rest comes from does not pass.
